## Findings

1. **Database topology was not traced sufficiently.** The document's section
   2.3 and summary infer a globally shared learner users table from packages/db's
   email uniqueness. apps/api-server/src/modules/auth/brand-db.ts already selects
   @quiz/db-skillup and @quiz/db-rth for the respective brands. Their connectors
   use DATABASE_URL_SKILLUP and DATABASE_URL_RTH respectively (both index.ts:30).
   A unique constraint applies within its physical table, not across separate
   databases. Environment variable names prove intended connection routing, not
   deployed endpoints; do not inspect or expose secret values to resolve this.

2. **The actual bridge and ID producer were omitted.** LoginService imports
   @quiz/identity-bridge, calls syncUser with brand user.id as externalId and
   brand as platform, stores the resulting shadowUserId back on the brand user,
   and issues originalUserId=user.id with the bridge ID as shadowUserId.
   See login.service.ts:32-52 and :178-185; signup.service.ts:88-97.
   UserIdentityBridgeService looks up (externalId, platform) in @quiz/db-people
   and returns that shadow row's ID. That is direct evidence of an existing
   brand-local-to-SHC projection mapping, not evidence of administrator
   impersonation. Other flows still require tracing; do not universalize this
   observed login meaning.

3. **There is a real, different email-uniqueness concern.** The SHC people users
   schema itself has unique email (packages/db-people/src/schema/users.ts:10)
   AND a unique externalId/platform index (:26). The bridge inserts email for
   each platform-specific projection. This can conflict for equal emails across
   tenant projections if the deployed schema matches source. Trace signup/login
   failure behavior and migrations before proposing a narrow correction. Do not
   turn this concern into a claim that tenant learner databases must be redesigned.

4. **The session persistence conclusion is unproven.** packages/auth's
   SessionService takes an injected SessionRepository. Comparing its interface
   with packages/db's sessions table does not establish the active repository,
   its storage, or an exploitable missing tenant binding. Trace concrete adapters
   and call sites, or explicitly mark the path unresolved.

5. **Several decisions are premature or overstated.** Do not declare
   shadowUserId canonical for all consumers or plan userId removal before tracing
   their namespaces. Do not infer impersonation from names. ILS ownership must be
   server-authoritative, but cryptographically signing each ILS identifier is not
   mandatory where opaque IDs and server-side tenant/learner ownership checks
   suffice. Header trust versus JWT transport is an engineering recommendation
   to justify from existing verifier boundaries, not an arbitrary owner choice.
   Gemini's fifth decision also differs from Sonnet's section 7.5 (migration).

The architecture/global-identity contradiction and need for independent tenant
learner ownership remain valid. Equal IDs/emails across tenants must not cause
automatic linkage; they can still belong to the same human. Independent accounts
do not imply proof that they are different people.
