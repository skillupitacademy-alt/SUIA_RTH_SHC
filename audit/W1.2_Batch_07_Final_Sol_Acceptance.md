configured, hostname-aligned ALLOWED_ORIGINS path. It rejects malformed/noncanonical
Origins, userinfo, missing/null origin and the tested wrong scheme/port/tenant/substrings.

of NextRequest and any deployed ALLOWED_ORIGINS entries require deployment-specific
qualification. No environment configuration was edited by this review.

The audience/class gate excludes infra and SHC audiences before session count/mutation.
Tenant subject/brand parity and the legacy success/snapshot-count contract are preserved.
The engine distinguishes revocation-unknown from confirmed-revocation/presence failure
without changing canonical success shape. Cookie/deprecation metadata is not added.

The corrected state tests use independent brand-bound adapters, a foreign-tenant canary,
wrong-store sensitivity, commit-then-throw persistence, presence failure, real AuditService
