Verify actual deployed constraints and duplicate/soft-delete data; update every email lookup and conflict target first, add partial-write reconciliation/idempotent retry, then stage a separately approved migration with preflight, backup and rollback that refuses rollback to global uniqueness while duplicates remain. Equal email across different learner platforms must produce separate projections; ambiguous unscoped lookup must deny, not pick the first row. Same-platform collision, staff coexistence, write failure between projection/access/link, concurrent retries and deployment rollback need isolated real-consumer controls.
