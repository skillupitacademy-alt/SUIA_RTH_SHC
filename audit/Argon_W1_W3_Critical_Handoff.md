## C. Engineering Principles Adherence & Offline Migration / Rollback Design

1. **Software Engineering Principles (`FinalAechitecture.md` & `Architecture_Remediation_Execution_Plan.md`)**:
   - **SOLID & Separation of Concerns**: HTTP routes (`export/*/route.ts`, `auth/profile/route.ts`, `workers/*/route.ts`, `workflows.py`) handle transport, signature/principal extraction, and status codes; domain services (`OwnershipRBACService`, `ExportEngine`, `WorkflowGovernanceService`, `HierarchySyncService`, `RemediationService`) enforce business invariants; repositories and lease adapters (`TutorialProgressRepository`, `createDurableEventLeaseStore`, `worker-lease.ts`) encapsulate SQL CAS and Redis Lua atomic transitions.
   - **DRY**: Shared lease ownership primitives are consolidated in `packages/events/src/consumer.ts` (`DurableEventLeaseStore`, `CLAIM_EVENT_LEASE_LUA`, `COMPLETE_IF_OWNER_LUA`, `RELEASE_IF_OWNER_LUA`) and `apps/realtutorialhub-web/src/app/api/workers/worker-lease.ts` (`acquireWorkerLease`, `completeWorkerLeaseIfOwner`, `releaseWorkerLeaseIfOwner`); shared export brand provenance resolvers are consolidated in `apps/api-server/src/lib/export/exportTypes.ts`; shared canonical option-ID scoring resolvers are consolidated in `apps/api-server/src/modules/question/question-contract.ts`.
   - **KISS & YAGNI**: Removed test-framework introspection (`isMockSetWithExplicitReturn`) in favor of a single explicit adapter contract (`DurableEventLeaseStore` / `ConsumerRedisLike`).
2. **Historical Compatibility, Quarantine & Expand–Contract Rollback Design (`TC06`, `TC07`, `TC17`, `TC18`, `TC20-B`)**:
   - **Tenant Provenance Quarantine (`TC06`, `TC09`, `TC11`, `TC12`)**: Rows or cached artifacts lacking explicit `brand`/`tenantId` or carrying conflicting brand fields fail closed (`403` / `undefined`) at the application boundary without destructive deletion or guessed tenant assignment. Physical backfill follows a 3-phase expand–contract plan: (Phase 1: nullable column expansion + application dual-write of verified `tenantId`; Phase 2: offline deterministic backfill of historical rows from verified user-store provenance, quarantining unresolvable rows into an audit view; Phase 3: `NOT NULL` constraint activation with instant rollback via constraint drop if anomalies arise).
   - **Question Option-ID Compatibility (`TC17` / Sol `D5`)**: Versioned submissions (`v1:ids:...`) match strictly against canonical `option.id` (`strictIdOnly`). Legacy unversioned submissions resolve unambiguously when display text is unique across options and fail closed (`Ambiguous unversioned option text in question snapshot; canonical option ID is required`) if duplicate display text exists, preventing historical grade corruption.
   - **Event Idempotency Compatibility (`TC20-B` / Sol `D7`)**: `parseIdempotencyRecord` supports both `sha256:<hex>` and legacy `fnv1a:<hex>` digests on `completed:` records while requiring unique `leaseToken` fencing on all `pending:` / `processing:` claims.
