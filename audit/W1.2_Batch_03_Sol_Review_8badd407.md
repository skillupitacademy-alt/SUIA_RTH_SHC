### F2 - New tenant policy changes SHC/infra/default-DB behavior (blocking)

The service binds UserRepository at lines 53-55 and executes strict/permissive
eligibility for every brand/audience, not just skillup/realtutorialhub user/admin.
The infra branch (305-310) forces isAdmin=true and uses newly queried roles/email.

These are changes to explicitly excluded principal paths, not preservation.

Create one explicit scope predicate for the new tenant policy. Outside it, preserve
the accepted-base outputs/claims/audience and avoid all new tenant user/role lookups
or dependencies. Do not weaken crypto verification or introduce a duplicate
authentication framework. Compare actual behavior with 8f419b0f; do not guess the
correct infra behavior or promote infra merely because the requested audience says
infra. Keep specialized sub/family tokens separate, without a blanket new SHC ban.

Reproduction: an infra/skillhubcore request with accepted verifier output was
expected to refresh without consulting the tenant eligibility mock. It instead
rejected OUT_OF_SCOPE_TENANT_LOOKUP. Existing route test only omits the infra cookie
and observes an early missing-cookie error; it never tests successful infra
dispatch through the actual service or preservation of its minted claims.
