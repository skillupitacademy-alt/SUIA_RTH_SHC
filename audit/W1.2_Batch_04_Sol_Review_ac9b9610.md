### R4 - Replay classification and timestamp ordering (REFRESH)

A revoked row is not proof of consumption: revokeById/revokeAll/revokeToken also set revoked. No consumedAt, revokedAt or reason exists in the generic row. lastUsedAt is not reliable rotation time: it is also modified outside CAS. CAS returning null can reflect a race, revocation or expiry between checks; it does not prove benign concurrency or theft. Replace 5s/10s claims with unapproved policy hypotheses and explicit evidence limits. Do not classify a recent retry as benign solely from timing. Preserve fail-closed rejection and no automatic user-wide revocation.

Section 4's deterministic final lastUsedAt=T2 example is incorrect: consumeToken also writes lastUsedAt at its own later time. Interleaving controls the final value. Correct conclusion: earlier autocommitted metadata can survive later failure, but no live PostgreSQL execution or exact final timestamp was demonstrated here. Rename 'Uncommitted State Update' accordingly.

SHC AdminGuard handles/cancels that event. 'Always forced to login' is false; document applicable caller/guard state. globalRefreshPromise deduplicates within one JS module realm, not across browser tabs or server processes.

Do not assert deployed flag values, browser cookie acceptance or network-failure reproduction. Audit insert failures are ordinarily swallowed; avoid the absolute claim that no possible production audit failure can ever affect response completion.
