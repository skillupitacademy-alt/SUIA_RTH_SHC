## Proposed vocabulary to review, not published runtime contracts

- platform/tenant context: evaluate existing platform or brand fields first;
  identify their authoritative validation and mappings. The logical tenant
  namespace for learners is skillup or realtutorialhub, never shared. tenantId
  is a discussion term only unless a separate field is justified and approved.
- learnerId: owned by one learner tenant. Its lookup/authorization key includes
  verified tenant context; equal IDs across tenants identify independent learners.
- authenticated principal: issuer plus subject (or a proven equivalent authority
  binding). principalId alone is not globally authoritative without its issuer.
- service identity: authenticated calling workload; distinguish actor service
  from any delegated learner. Tenant entitlement and allowed action are separate.
- SHC administrator: staff principal with SHC administrative permissions;
  administrative role is not automatic learner impersonation or tenant access.
- authentication session versus learning session: separate authorities and
  lifecycles. Neither identifier is interchangeable with learner identity.
- audit actor: server-derived actor type, authority and scoped ID; record service
  actor and delegated subject separately when applicable.

One SHC request requires verified caller authority, entitled tenant, permitted
action and authorized resource scope. Service-only requests need no invented
learner identity. For public/shared resources explicitly record the applicable
resource policy; do not fabricate learner ownership. Raw browser user/tenant
headers, email equality and a bare learner ID do not establish authority.
