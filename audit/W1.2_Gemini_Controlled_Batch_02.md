## Why this batch is bounded to design

Next authority changes may reject legacy learner/admin/service tokens and alter
account-status or role behavior. The source inventory identifies gaps, but does
not yet define compatibility for every issuer/consumer. Produce concrete contracts
and minimal implementation scopes first. This avoids repeating a wide auth rewrite.
