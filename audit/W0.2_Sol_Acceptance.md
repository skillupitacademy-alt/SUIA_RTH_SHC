## Correction to the build explanation

The successful output identifies **Next.js 16.1.6 (Turbopack)**. The local
installed `next/dist/lib/bundler.js` checks the truthiness of
`process.env.TURBOPACK`; the nonempty string `"0"` selects Turbopack, not
Webpack. Explicit `--webpack` selects Webpack. Therefore the claim that
`TURBOPACK=0` disabled Turbopack or established the stall's root cause is
incorrect. The successful run is valid completed-build evidence, but the
cause of the earlier intermittent stalls remains unproven. No build-policy
change is made as acceptance bookkeeping.

Earlier diagnostic reports are historical evidence, not authoritative root
cause conclusions. This acceptance supersedes their bundler explanation.
