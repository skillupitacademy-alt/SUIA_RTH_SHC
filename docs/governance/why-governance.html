Governance isn't bureaucracy.
It's infrastructure.

Just like you wouldn't build a hospital on sand because "foundations take too long", you don't run a production exam platform on any types and console.log.

The 30 sessions (~15-20 hours) you invest now will save hundreds of hours of debugging, hundreds of user complaints, and one catastrophic data incident that you can't recover from.
