# Report: Nature of Knowledge (The Mapping Trinity)

## 🎯 Objective
To distinguish between theoretical knowledge and practical execution ability.

## 📊 Dimension Mapping
- **Primary Source**: `results_by_dimension`
- **Dimension Type**: `mapping_type`
- **Types**: `conceptual`, `technical`, `practical`

---

## 📈 Analysis Views

### 1. Knowledge Nature Split
A 3-way distribution analysis:

- **Conceptual**: Mastery of "The Why" (Theory, Design Patterns).
- **Technical**: Mastery of "The What" (Syntax, API signatures).
- **Practical**: Mastery of "The How" (Debugging, Implementation, Performance).

### 2. The "Applied Gap" Index
Compares **Conceptual Score** vs. **Practical Score**. 
- A high gap indicates a candidate who understands theory well but struggles with hands-on labs/simulation questions.

---

## 📝 Business Insight
"Candidate is 'Hands-on Heavy' (High Practical), showing instant value in feature delivery despite lower scores in abstract 'Conceptual' theory."
