# Design System Rulebook for Your Products

Based on these brochure pages, I recommend adopting these rules consistently across your websites, admin portals, tutorial engine, and marketing materials:

1. **Use `#0B1B3D` as the structural colour**—headers, navigation, titles, primary text, dashboards, and containers. It conveys trust and keeps interfaces readable.
2. **Reserve `#f54a8d` for emphasis**—calls to action, highlighted words, active states, important numbers, badges, progress indicators, and key benefits. This ensures pink remains attention-grabbing.
3. **Keep backgrounds predominantly white**, using subtle grey or blue tints only to separate sections or cards.
4. **Alternate icon colours** (blue → pink → blue → pink) in grids of cards to create rhythm without adding visual clutter.
5. **Use semantic colours independently of brand colours**: green for success, red for errors, amber/orange for warnings, and blue for information. Don't force brand colours into status messaging.

Following these principles will give every product in the SkillUp ecosystem—from brochures to the Tutorial Engine and admin dashboards—a consistent, premium enterprise identity while maintaining excellent readability and visual hierarchy.
